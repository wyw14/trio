`trio.run_process` and `trio.lowlevel.open_process` now snapshot the
``env`` mapping (copied at call time) and resolve and check ``cwd`` before
spawning, so each child process is isolated from later modifications by the
caller or by sibling processes. Missing working directories, inaccessible
working directories, and process-start failures are now reported as distinct
errors; calls that don't specify ``env`` or ``cwd`` keep the usual
inheritance behavior.
